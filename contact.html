Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and elopements, I recommend booking 6-12 months in advance. For portraits and other sessions, 2-4 weeks is typically sufficient.

How long until I receive my photos?

Weddings are delivered within 4-6 weeks. Portrait and lifestyle sessions are typically ready within 2-3 weeks.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! I love destination weddings and am available to travel anywhere. Travel fees vary by location.

What is included in a session?

All sessions include a pre-session consultation, professional editing, an online gallery for viewing and downloading, and a print release for personal use.
