Good to Know

Picnic Proposal Questions

Do we need a permit?

Events held in San Diego public parks require a park use permit, and yes, that includes Sunset Cliffs, which sits on the approved city shoreline park list. We can handle the whole permit process for you for an added charge, so you never have to deal with the city. Most spots need about 15 days of lead time, and the most popular shoreline parks can need more, so the earlier you inquire the more spots stay open.

Will the surprise stay a surprise?

Yes. We plan everything over text and email so nothing appears where it shouldn't, the setup is finished before you arrive, and I photograph from a distance until the moment happens.

What does it cost all together?

Hidden Picnics proposal setups start at $750, and my proposal photography is $650, so most picnic proposals land between $1,400 and $2,000 all in before taxes and any permit. Just want the photography without a styled setup? That is $650 on its own. Proposal setups cost more than picnics because they include the romantic staging itself, the candles, florals, and decor, plus the coordination of keeping it all a secret. Want film too? Photo and film proposals typically land between $2,900 and $3,050 with the setup included. The film is a cinematic highlight of about a minute, plus vertical clips for sharing, delivered with your photos within 2 to 3 weeks. Want to hear the moment itself? Live audio of your proposal can be added to the film for an additional charge. In a hurry? Rush delivery and same-week sneak peeks are available for an added charge. Budget a little extra on top of any package: California sales tax adds about $25 to $70 on the setup, and a city park permit typically runs about $25 to $100 more. The estimator above shows your exact combination.

How does booking and payment work?

One inquiry starts everything. Photography is covered by my contract with a 50% retainer, and Hidden Picnics sends their own agreement with a 50% deposit for the setup, with their balance due 72 hours before the event. Both deposits hold your date and are non-refundable on outright cancellation, and weather reschedules move everything to your new date instead. Two signatures, one coordinated plan, and I keep both sides in sync.

Visiting San Diego?

Everything is planned remotely over text, email, and a free call, from the exact spot to the timing of the light, so you just land and show up.

What about weather?

If unsafe weather moves in, my photography reschedules at no charge. Hidden Picnics watches the forecast too, and their policy is to reschedule the setup to a future date when the rain chance reaches 40% or more, with indoor alternatives offered where possible. Your date moves, and we coordinate the new plan together, one conversation, not two.

Where can we do it?

Sunset Cliffs, La Jolla, Coronado, Mission Bay, and grassy parks overlooking the ocean are the most loved spots. Anywhere in San Diego County works, with no photography travel fee inside the county.
