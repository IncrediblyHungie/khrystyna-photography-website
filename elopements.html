Elopements

Barefoot, Windblown, and Completely You

No big production, no stiff poses. Just you two, or up to 15 of your favorite people, somewhere beautiful: toes in the sand at Cardiff, golden light at Sunset Cliffs, or a quiet overlook you love. I'll help you pick the spot and the time, then quietly document a day that actually feels like yours. Wondering about permits or an officiant?
