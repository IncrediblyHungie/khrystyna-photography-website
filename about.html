About Me

The story behind the lens

Hi, I'm Khrystyna

I'm a San Diego-based photographer specializing in maternity, portrait, lifestyle, and elopement photography. Photography has always been more than just a career for me — it's my way of seeing the world and connecting with the people I photograph.

What I love most about photography is noticing the little details — the way light falls on a face, a genuine smile, an unguarded moment between two people. I see photography as a mix of storytelling and artistry, and my goal is to create images that feel both authentic and timeless.

My approach is simple: I want you to feel comfortable, celebrated, and truly yourself in front of the camera. Whether we're capturing the joy of expecting a new baby, the intimacy of an elopement, or the essence of who you are in a portrait session, I'm here to make the experience feel natural and enjoyable.

I believe photography should be an experience — not just a product. When you work with me, you're not just getting beautiful images; you're getting someone who genuinely cares about your story and wants to tell it in the most heartfelt way possible.
