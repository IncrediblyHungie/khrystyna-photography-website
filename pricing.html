No Surprises

How Booking Works

Every booking is covered by a signed contract, so you know exactly what is reserved and what is owed before anything is due.

1. Check your date

Send an inquiry or book a free 30 minute call. I confirm availability and answer questions before you commit to anything.

2. Sign and reserve

A signed contract plus a 50% retainer reserves your date. The remaining balance is due 14 days before your wedding or session.

3. Plan together

We build your timeline, walk through locations and permits, and coordinate with your planner or venue as the day gets closer.

4. Receive your gallery

Every gallery is delivered within 2 to 3 weeks in a private online gallery, weddings and sessions alike.

Plans change. You may reschedule once with at least 30 days' notice and your retainer carries over. Outdoor sessions affected by unsafe weather are rescheduled at no extra charge. See booking and cancellation questions answered. Full terms are in the contract you receive before any payment is due.
