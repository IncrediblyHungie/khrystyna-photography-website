Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

There's no set rule. Some couples book a year out, others reach out a few weeks before. Just send me your date and I'll let you know right away whether it's still open.

How long until I receive my photos?

Every gallery, weddings and sessions alike, is delivered within 2-3 weeks.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Absolutely! I love destination weddings and am available to travel anywhere. Travel fees vary by location, and your exact travel cost is quoted in writing within 24 hours of your inquiry, as one flat line item that never changes after booking. Travel time never counts against your coverage hours, which start when Khrystyna arrives at your venue.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A 50% retainer is due upon signing to reserve your date, with the remaining balance due 14 days before your wedding or session. For proposals and engagements that is just $325 to lock your date, and for portrait sessions it is $250.

I'm planning a surprise proposal. How does that work?

Quietly! We plan everything over text or email, you approve when and how I reach out, and on the day I stay out of sight until the yes. If a surprise needs to move on short notice, tell me right away and I'll always do my best to move with you when my schedule allows.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

The 50% retainer is non-refundable, since it reserves your date and covers planning time. You may reschedule once with at least 30 days' notice, subject to availability, and your retainer carries over to the new date. For outdoor sessions affected by unsafe weather, we will reschedule at no extra charge. If a wedding is cancelled within 30 days of the date, the remaining balance is also due. In the rare event that Khrystyna must cancel, you receive a full refund including the retainer, and she will help you find a qualified replacement photographer.

Do you bring backup equipment?

Always. I bring backup gear to every shoot, so your day is fully covered no matter what.

What is included in a session?

All sessions include a pre-session consultation, professional editing, an online gallery for viewing and downloading, and a print release for personal use.
