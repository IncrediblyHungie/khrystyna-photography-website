Step by Step

How a Surprise Proposal Works

1. We Plan Quietly

Everything happens over text or email, and you approve when and how I reach out. We pick the spot, the exact timing for the best light, and where I'll be standing before she ever arrives.

2. I Stay Invisible

I blend in like any other visitor with a camera, out of sight until the moment. She won't know I'm there until the yes. Foggy or windy day? We watch the forecast together and shift timing or location at no charge.

3. The Moment, Then More

I capture the reaction, the ring, and the happy tears, then we roll into a mini engagement session. Going big with an arch, musicians, or friends hiding nearby? Tell me the plan and I'll help cue everyone.

Photography is $650. Want cinematic film of the moment too? A second shooter joins so photos and film are captured at once: photo + film together from $2,150. In a hurry to share? Rush delivery and same-week sneak peeks are available for an additional charge, just ask when you inquire.
