About Me

Hi, I'm Khrystyna

I'm a San Diego-based photographer specializing in wedding, elopement, maternity, and lifestyle photography. My approach is simple: I want you to feel comfortable, celebrated, and truly yourself in front of the camera.

What I love most about photography is noticing the little details — the way light falls, a genuine smile, an unguarded moment between two people. I see it as a mix of storytelling and artistry, creating images that feel authentic and timeless.
